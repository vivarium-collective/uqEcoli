📊 How to Read Sobol Indices

Si (First-Order): The fraction of total output variance explained by a parameter alone. A high Si means this parameter directly controls the output — if you could fix it, you'd eliminate that much uncertainty.

STi (Total-Order): The fraction explained by a parameter including all its interactions with other parameters. The gap STi − Si reveals how much a parameter acts through interactions rather than independently.

V(Y) = ∑i Vi + ∑i<j Vij + ... + V12...d

Rule of thumb: Parameters with high STi are the most promising targets for experimental measurement — reducing uncertainty in these parameters will most effectively tighten model predictions. Ranking consistency across strategies (left) builds confidence in the findings.
